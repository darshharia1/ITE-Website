Why ITE LaunchPad

Everything You Need to Launch

Milestone Tracking

6-phase structured milestones from idea validation to final pitch, with real-time progress tracking and mentor reviews.

Expert Mentors

7 industry-expert mentors personally assigned to each team, providing structured feedback and startup guidance.

Role-Based System

Assign real C-Suite roles — CEO, CTO, COO, CFO, CMO — to simulate actual startup team dynamics and accountability.

Structured Submissions

Submit milestone deliverables directly through the platform for mentor review and approval with feedback loops.

Team Formation Engine

CEOs can search and invite unassigned students based on skills, creating balanced, complementary teams.

Admin Analytics

Professor/Admin dashboard with full program oversight — student metrics, team progress, and submission management.
